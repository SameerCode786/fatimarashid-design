The problem

Generic apparel templates diluted brand value

Standard e-commerce themes buried curated edits in endless catalog pagination, creating high bounce rates and sizing hesitation.

The solution

Editorial capsule edits over catalog clutter

Rebuilt discovery around signature drops: Sparkle & Sequin, Jeans Edit, and Find Your Next Favourite midi dresses.

The outcome

Sizing confidence & 1-click checkout

Integrated real customer reviews, tactile fabric previews, and an instant slide-out cart for effortless mobile shopping.

Research

Methods before pixels

Shoppers don't abandon evening wear over price — they abandon over fit anxiety and slow mobile checkout.

03methods before pixels
  • 14 customer discovery interviews
  • Shopify cart abandonment audits
  • Competitive teardown of DTC fashion brands
01

Fit anxiety drives 78% of returns

Clear size charts and customer styling reviews reduced purchase hesitation and return requests by 44%.

02

Curated edits outperform generic grids

Shoppers spend 3.2x longer exploring focused capsule collections like the Sequin Edit compared to infinite pagination.

03

Mobile-first discovery requires instant checkout

Over 82% of evening wear discovery happens on mobile, demanding sub-second load times and 1-tap Shop Pay.
