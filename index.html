Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about brand identity, product design, Shopify development, deliverables, and how we collaborate.

Have a specific question?talk@fatimarashid.design →
[01]How does the four-week timeline work?

Week 1 focuses on strategy and positioning, including business discovery, audience research, and project planning. Week 2 focuses on brand identity, visual direction, typography, and brand guidelines. Week 3 focuses on product UX/UI and Shopify storefront design, including responsive layouts and shopping flows. Week 4 focuses on Shopify development, functionality checks, responsive testing, and launch preparation. The exact timeline depends on the project's scope and requirements.

[02]What is included in the Brand-to-URL delivery?

You receive a comprehensive visual identity system (Figma library and documented guidelines), production-ready responsive website codebase deployed on your infrastructure, complete SEO metadata, social launch assets, and 30 days of post-launch iteration support.

[03]Do you design and code, or just design?

Both. I design in Figma and engineer custom Shopify storefronts and web code using Liquid, modern JavaScript, and responsive design systems. Because the same person architects the system from brief to code, the design on your screen never gets compromised or lost in translation between separate teams.

[04]How are projects priced and invoiced?

Fixed scope, fixed price, milestone invoicing. Engagements start with clear terms and transparent deliverables. No surprise change orders, no unpredictable hourly billing. You know exactly what ships and when it goes live.

[05]Can we collaborate if we already have an existing brand or app?

Yes. Many engagements begin with a friction and UX audit of an existing SaaS product or legacy website. We pinpoint where users drop off, preserve your established brand equity, and rebuild the interface and design system to scale.

[06]What happens after launch?

Every project includes a 30-day warranty and post-launch iteration window for fine-tuning. For ongoing needs, flexible monthly retainers are available covering new feature UI/UX, conversion rate optimization, and brand asset expansions.
