The challenge

Generic fitness stores lacked brand power

Most activewear stores look like generic templates without athletic energy, leading to high bounce rates and zero brand recall.

The brand move

High-impact athletic identity & curated collections

Repositioned Curved Brand around power, movement, and durability with bold typography, high-energy imagery, and seamless category curation.

The outcome

High conversion & 1,000+ satisfied athletes

Delivered an end-to-end Shopify experience that unites men's training vests, women's seamless yoga sets, and gym hardware with 1-click checkout.
