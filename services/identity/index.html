What is insideFive parts, built in order
01Part onePositioning and messaging

What a brand identity includes.

Part one

Positioning and messaging

The idea the brand stands for, who it is for, and the words it uses. Everything visual is built on top of it.

  • Positioning statement
  • Audience and competitor map
  • Messaging and voice
Part two

Logo system

A mark that works at every size, with the lockups, spacing and colour versions a team needs.

  • Primary mark and lockups
  • Icon and favicon versions
  • Clear space and minimum sizes
Part three

Colour and type

A palette and a type pairing chosen for contrast, accessibility and the voice of the brand.

  • Colour palette with usage rules
  • Type pairing and scale
Codestreaks: a 4.8 out of 5 trust score.
Part four

Custom pattern

A pattern drawn for the brand, so surfaces, packaging and social posts look like only this brand.

  • Pattern design
  • Usage on surfaces and backgrounds
Begum: a custom Mughal-inspired pattern.
Part five

Brand guidelines

A slide-format guide that explains how to use everything, so any designer or developer applies it correctly.

  • Slide-format guidelines
  • Do and do not examples
Begum: 38-slide brand guidelines.

Before you writeShort answers

Questions about brand identity.

What do I receive at the end?

Positioning and messaging, a logo system, a colour and type system, a custom pattern and brand guidelines in slide format. Social and print templates are available as launch assets.

How long does it take?

The identity is the first half of a four-week Brand-to-URL build, delivered together with the website. If you need the identity on its own, we scope the timeline on a call.

Do you also build the website?

Yes. The website is designed from the identity and built to launch, so the two never drift apart. One invoice covers both.

Who do you work with?

Pre-seed to Series A SaaS and AI founders, and brands that need an identity people recognise and trust. It is a solo practice, so you work directly with me.
