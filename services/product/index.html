What is insideFive parts, from friction to result
01Part oneAudit

What product and UX design includes.

Part one

Flow and friction audit

A map of the journey, with the steps where users slow down, hesitate or give up marked clearly.

  • Journey and flow map
  • Friction points ranked
  • Decision points reviewed
Part two

Flow design

The same goal reached in fewer steps. Every choice that does not help the user finish is removed or simplified.

  • Redesigned flows
  • Simpler choices
  • Clear next steps
Strong Rental Cars Qatar: six booking steps cut to three.
Part three

Interface design

Screens for web and mobile that make the new flows easy to follow, in your brand.

  • Screen design for web and mobile
  • Interaction details
  • States and edge cases
Part four

Design system

A shared set of components and tokens, so the product stays consistent as the team grows it.

  • Component library
  • Colour, type and spacing tokens
Part five

Measured outcomes

Results are checked against the friction you started with, so the change shows up in the numbers.

  • A baseline to compare against
  • Measures that track the result
Petrozen ERP: 58% fewer accounting errors, 46% less time on invoice entry.

Before you writeShort answers

Questions about product design.

What do I receive at the end?

A friction audit, redesigned flows, interface designs for web and mobile, a design system, and a baseline with measures so the change can be seen in the numbers.

Which products do you work on?

SaaS and AI products, including internal tools such as an ERP. The best fit is a product that already works but costs its users effort.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the product and the flows involved, so we scope the timeline together on a call.

Do I work with a team or with you?

With me. It is a solo practice, so the person you brief is the person who designs the product.
