Before you writeShort answers

Questions about Shopify development.

What is included in a Shopify store?

A complete, launch-ready Shopify storefront: store strategy and navigation structure, responsive storefront layouts (desktop, tablet, mobile), product and collection configuration, cart and checkout flow, theme customization, necessary app integrations, and comprehensive cross-device testing before go-live.

Do you design and develop the Shopify store?

Yes, both. I design the storefront in Figma around your brand identity and products, then build it directly on Shopify using custom Liquid, theme development, and responsive styling. Because one person handles both, nothing gets lost in handoff.

Can you customize an existing Shopify theme?

Yes. Whether starting from a clean Shopify OS 2.0 base theme or customizing an existing theme your business already uses, I can rebuild layouts, add custom sections, and adjust styling to perfectly match your brand.

Can you help with product pages and collections?

Yes. I design and configure high-converting product detail pages, organized collection grids, filtering systems, and featured product modules tailored to showcase your catalogue effectively.

Can you integrate Shopify apps?

Yes. I can configure essential Shopify apps for reviews, email marketing, upsells, analytics, inventory management, and customer support, ensuring they integrate seamlessly without slowing down your store.

Is the Shopify store responsive?

Completely. Every storefront is engineered mobile-first, ensuring smooth navigation, clear imagery, and an effortless checkout experience across phones, tablets, and wide desktop displays.

How long does Shopify development take?

A typical Shopify store build takes four weeks from initial strategy to live launch. When paired with brand identity in a full Brand-to-Store engagement, both are delivered together within a structured four-week sprint.

Can I combine brand identity and Shopify development?

Yes. Through the Brand-to-Store service, your brand identity (positioning, logo system, color tokens, typography) and your Shopify storefront are designed and built as one unified system, under one invoice and timeline.
