Before you writeShort answers

Questions about launch assets.

What do I receive at the end?

A launch plan, social media templates, print collateral and the files to reuse them, all built from the same system as your brand and website.

Do I need an existing brand identity?

The assets are built from your identity. If you do not have one yet, the identity and the assets can be designed together as one system.

Which channels do you cover?

Social media and print. We agree the exact channels and formats on a call, based on where your launch needs to show up.

How long does it take?

It depends on how many assets you need, so we scope the timeline together on a call.
