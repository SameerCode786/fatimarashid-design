How Much Does SaaS WebsiteDesign Cost in 2026?

An honest pricing breakdown for SaaS founders, landing pages, full marketing sites, and brand-to-URL systems.

In 2026, SaaS website design typically costs $2,500-$7,500 for a focused landing or small marketing site, roughly $12K-$24K for a full brand-and-site system from an independent senior operator, and $20K-$120K+ when a multi-person agency runs strategy, design, and production in parallel.

Those ranges are not universal laws. They are what I see across indie operators and boutique agencies serving early SaaS. Your number moves with scope, proof assets, engineering inclusion, and how many decision-makers can veto the work.

[ H_02 ]·#anchor

What you are actually buying

  • 01Landing page, message, layout, proof, CTA, basic SEO metadata. Fastest path to testing demand.
  • 02Marketing site, homepage, product, pricing, about, contact, blog index. Needs IA and consistent system.
  • 03Brand-to-URL system, positioning, identity, site, and often production code. Highest coherence, highest scope.
  • 04Product + site, marketing and in-app UX designed together. Best when activation and conversion are both weak.
[ H_04 ]·#anchor

Price drivers that matter more than fonts

  1. 01.Whether strategy is included or assumed
  2. 02.Custom illustration / motion / 3D vs product screenshots
  3. 03.CMS and engineering (design-only vs Next.js ship)
  4. 04.Number of stakeholders who must approve
  5. 05.Timeline compression (48-hour and two-week sprints cost urgency)
[ H_06 ]·#anchor

Independent operator ranges (this studio)

My published tracks start roughly here: brand strategy from $12K, brand systems from $18K, web engineering from $22K, product design from $24K, fixed scope, fixed price. Smaller packages and sprints exist for focused surfaces; see services and the current sprint offer. The point is predictability: you should know the exit before the invoice.

[ H_08 ]·#anchor

Agency ranges (what founders report)

SaaS-focused agencies often quote $20K-$60K for a marketing site rebuild and much more when brand, product, and ongoing retainers stack. Parallel HQ-class studios can exceed $100K for broader product engagements. Higher price can be rational at scale, it is rarely rational for a pre-PMF landing test.

[ H_11 ]·#anchor

How to budget without wasting a quarter

  • 01Fund a diagnosis before a full rebuild
  • 02Prefer fixed scope over open retainers for first ship
  • 03Budget engineering if you need a URL, not only Figma
  • 04Keep decision rights with one founder-level owner
  • 05Reuse the system for product UI, do not buy brand twice

If you are still choosing a hiring model, read freelance vs agency for SaaS. If you need the full design OS, start with the SaaS Design Operating System.
