OriginWhere the method comes from

I failed a physics exam. Then I passed by drawing the system first.

That habit never left. Physics taught me to look for the structure under a problem before touching it, and I carry that into every brand and every website: before I design a logo or a layout, I draw the system the business actually runs on.

Most of the founders I work with share one problem. The brand was made by one person and the website by another, and customers feel the gap between them. I deliver the identity and a production-ready website together, as one system and one invoice, with no handoff between the two.

I treat design as business infrastructure, not aesthetics. The work I value most removes friction nobody reported, and I measure it by what changes for the business.

Design is business infrastructure, not aesthetics.Fatima Rashid

Principles

Four rules I design by, each backed by work I can point to.

4rules, no exceptions

Friction before polish

I look for the friction a founder cannot see before I touch a visual: the extra step, the unclear label, the page that asks for too much.

  • Flow audit
  • Decision points
  • Removal first
Strong Rental Cars Qatar: six booking steps cut to three.

One system, not two deliverables

Brand and website are designed against the same strategy, so nothing is translated between teams and nothing drifts apart.

  • One strategy
  • One timeline
  • One invoice
Begum: identity, pattern, 38-slide guidelines, web, social and print.

Design as infrastructure

A design is judged by what it changes in the business: fewer errors, faster work, a brand people recognise.

  • Measured outcomes
  • Plain language
Petrozen ERP: 58% fewer accounting errors.

Proof over claims

I show results from the work instead of adjectives, and I only report numbers I can source.

  • Sourced numbers
  • Named projects
Codestreaks: 127% increase in brand recognition.
The physics lensThree ideas I use daily

Physics, applied to brands and websites.

Systems thinking

A brand, a website and a product are one system. I draw it before I design any part of it.

Decision entropy

Every extra choice on a page raises the cost of deciding. I remove choices until the next step is obvious.

Friction reduction

Friction is any step that costs the customer effort and gives nothing back. I find it, then remove it.
